Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

Atomic Finds ATX respects your privacy. We only collect basic contact information (such as your name, email address, phone number, and delivery zip code) when you voluntarily submit an inquiry form or sign up for The Dispatch newsletter.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to coordinate furniture adoptions, schedule Austin local pickups/deliveries, respond to item inquiries, and dispatch weekly restock alerts. We NEVER sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

3. Newsletter & Unsubscribing

If you sign up for The Dispatch, you will receive one email per week featuring newly landed 1970s rattan and bamboo finds. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any email or by contacting atomicfindsatx@gmail.com.
