## Heurio Design System & Brand Guidelines
- **Copy & Content**:
  - Never invent or fabricate reviews or contact info.
  - Pull actual reviews and links from the official Facebook Marketplace profile above.
  - Website copy from `atomicfindsatx.store` may be referenced, but **do not** bring over galaxy/space-theme elements or copy.
- **Logos & Branding**:
  - The Monogram belongs in the footer, prominently sized.
  - The Primary Logo is located inside `design-system/assets/logos/`.
- **Components & Layout**:
  - **Passport Card**: Intended specifically for buyers/provenance, not for generic product cards. Products need real descriptions.
  - **Photo Galleries**: Use fullwidth photo galleries instead of single small photo containers.
  - **Card Layout & Expandable Cards**: Cards should stack vertically or expand cleanly (e.g. expand to the side on click) rather than squeezing into cramped side-by-side grids with small photos.
  - **Buttons & Controls**: Keep buttons responsive and appropriately sized (avoid oversized/fat buttons). Provide compact/clean toggle buttons for UI controls like language switchers.
  - **About Section**: Must feature information about the owner.
