2. Adoptions & Hold Policy

Every vintage rattan or bamboo piece we discover is sold as an adoption. Since these are unique historical finds, all items are sold on a first-come, first-served basis. We can hold a piece for up to 24 hours once a deposit or full payment is completed.

3. Local Pickup & Delivery

Local pickup is arranged in South Austin, Texas upon purchase. Curbside drop-off is available around the greater Austin metro area for a small fee depending on distance and item size.

4. Restorations & Care

All products represent restored vintage bamboo and rattan structures. While we inspect every piece under the supervision of Nacho (Chief Curiosity Officer & Good Boy), these items carry natural retro character, wear, and history. We recommend indoor care and avoiding prolonged direct sun exposure to guarantee their preservation for another fifty years.
