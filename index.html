The Person Behind The Finds

Hi, I'm Jennyfer.

I'm the one behind Atomic Finds ATX—a one-woman search for vintage furniture and décor with warmth, shape, and a little personality.

I look for old, unusual, and beautifully made pieces that still have something to say. Some need a bit of attention before they are ready for their next chapter; others arrive with their character already intact. Either way, each find is selected, photographed, and shared with clear details about its condition, dimensions, and vintage wear.

Atomic Finds is about giving memorable pieces a fresh place in the world—and helping you find the one that makes your room feel more like home.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Pickup logistics, vintage condition standards, and holds.

We are based in South Austin, Texas! Local pickup is arranged upon purchase—we'll coordinate a convenient time window with you directly.
Atomic Finds ATX is local Austin pickup only. We do not provide delivery or shipping services. All pieces are available for pickup in Austin by arrangement once purchased. If you need help transporting a larger piece, you are welcome to coordinate with a local moving service or courier.
We hunt down vibrant 1960s and 1970s vintage treasures—focusing heavily on bent rattan, sculptural bamboo, boho accent seating, and retro statement pieces.
All our pieces are authentic vintage, meaning they carry genuine retro character and history. We inspect and clean every find, noting any distinct vintage wear or patina directly in the listing.
Due to high demand on vintage drops, items are sold on a first-confirmed basis. We can hold a piece for up to 24 hours once a deposit or full payment is completed.
Follow us on Instagram (@atomicfindsatx) for previews and story claims, or browse active listings directly on Facebook Marketplace under Jennyfer Gomez.
Because items are unique vintage finds, all sales are generally final once picked up. However, if an unexpected issue arises that wasn't noted, reach out immediately and we'll make it right.
