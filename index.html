Our Story

Restored by Hand for the Modern Home

Atomic Finds ATX is a one-woman operation, start to finish. Jennyfer sources every piece herself, then restores each one by hand. Her eye is the whole business: an instinct for the pieces worth saving, and the patience to bring 1970s rattan and bamboo back to life.

No warehouses, no mass production — just Jennyfer, a workshop, and a genuine love for mid-century craftsmanship. Every piece is hand-picked from Austin estate sales, auctions, and private collections, brought back to life before it ever reaches the shop floor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Pickup logistics, Austin delivery, vintage condition standards, and holds.

We are based in South Austin, Texas! Local pickup is arranged upon purchase—we'll coordinate a convenient time window with you directly.
Local curbside drop-off is available around the greater Austin metro area for a small fee depending on distance and item size. Reach out with your zip code for a quick quote!
We hunt down vibrant 1960s and 1970s vintage treasures—focusing heavily on bent rattan, sculptural bamboo, boho accent seating, and retro statement pieces.
All our pieces are authentic vintage, meaning they carry genuine retro character and history. We inspect and clean every find, noting any distinct vintage wear or patina directly in the listing.
Due to high demand on vintage drops, items are sold on a first-confirmed basis. We can hold a piece for up to 24 hours once a deposit or full payment is completed.
Follow us on Instagram (@atomicfindsatx) for previews and story claims, or browse active listings directly on Facebook Marketplace under Jennyfer Gomez.
Because items are unique vintage finds, all sales are generally final once picked up or delivered. However, if an unexpected issue arises that wasn't noted, reach out immediately and we’ll make it right.
