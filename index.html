Our Story

Found across time + space

Every piece in our collection is a tiny time machine — a chair with a past, a shelf with stories. We find them, name them, and give them a new chapter in your home.

We only bring home rattan and bamboo that has earned it: sound joints, honest wear, and the kind of silhouette nobody makes any more. Then it gets inspected, photographed in real light, and sent out with a passport telling you exactly where it came from.
