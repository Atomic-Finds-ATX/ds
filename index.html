Meet Jennyfer & Our Story

Restored by Hand for the Modern Home

Atomic Finds ATX is a one-woman operation, start to finish. Jennyfer sources every piece herself, then restores each one by hand. Her eye is the whole business: an instinct for the pieces worth saving, and the patience to bring 1970s rattan and bamboo back to life.

No warehouses, no mass production — just Jennyfer, a workshop, and a genuine love for mid-century craftsmanship. Every piece is hand-picked from Austin estate sales, auctions, and private collections, brought back to life before it ever reaches the shop floor.

The Process

How We Deliver

From Austin estate sales straight to your living room — handled with white-glove care.

Step 01

Browse & Select

Every piece in the collection is hand-picked by Jennyfer from Austin estate sales, markets, and private collections. What you see is what exists — no warehouse stock.

Step 02

Restored with Care

Each item is cleaned, oiled, photographed in natural light, and prepared with archival care. Rattan is fragile; we treat it like the heirloom it is.

Step 03

Local Austin Delivery

Delivered within the greater Austin metro — typically within 3–5 days. Carried inside, placed where you want it, and packaging taken away.

Step 04

100% Satisfaction

Every piece is backed by Jennyfer. If something arrives damaged or isn't as described, she'll make it right. No friction, no fine print.
