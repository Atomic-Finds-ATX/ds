Our Story

Curated with passion in Austin, Texas

Founded in Austin by Anthony, Atomic Finds ATX is built on a love for mid-century modern rattan, bamboo, and wicker design. Every piece is hand-selected from estate sales and local treasures across Central Texas, restored with care, and prepared for a new life in your home.

We only bring home rattan and bamboo that has earned it: sound joints, honest vintage wear, and silhouettes nobody manufactures today. Shop dog Nacho inspects every delivery to ensure maximum comfort and presence.
