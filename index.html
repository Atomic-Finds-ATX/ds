Meet Jennyfer

Since 2018, I've been obsessed with hunting down vintage treasures across Austin and beyond. What started as a personal passion for mid-century design has grown into a thriving community of collectors, designers, and nostalgia enthusiasts.

Each piece I find tells a story—from its original owner to the artisans who crafted it. My mission is to give these pieces a second life in homes where they'll be cherished and celebrated.

Local pickup available in Austin. Free delivery on orders over $500.
