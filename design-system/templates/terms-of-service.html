2. Adoptions & Hold Policy

Every vintage rattan or bamboo piece we discover is sold as an adoption. Since these are unique historical finds, all items are sold on a first-come, first-served basis. We require a 50% non-refundable deposit to hold any item for local pickup beyond 48 hours.

3. Showroom Bookings

Showroom visits are scheduled strictly by appointment. Please respect our curators' time and notify us at least 2 hours in advance of any schedule changes or cancellations.

4. Restorations & Care

All products represent restored vintage bamboo and rattan structures. While we inspect every piece aggressively under the supervision of Nacho, these items carry natural character, wear, and history. We recommend indoor care and avoiding prolonged direct sun exposure to guarantee their preservation for another fifty years.
