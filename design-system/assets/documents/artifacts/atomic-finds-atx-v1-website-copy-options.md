# Atomic Finds ATX — V1 Website Copy Options

**Brand voice:** Warm, curious, witty, colorful, and polished. Furniture is always the hero. The tone can be playful, but it should remain easy to understand, accurate, and useful to a buyer.

**Operating assumption for this copy:** Atomic Finds ATX is pickup-first and uses Facebook Marketplace as the current live-listing/sales channel. Any text marked **[CONFIRM]** must be confirmed with Jennyfer before publication.

---

## 1. Messaging Guardrails

### Use

- Curated, character-rich, warm, collected, found, adopted, next chapter, tiny time machine
- Vintage wear, patina, materials, dimensions, condition notes, thoughtfully prepared
- Austin pickup by arrangement
- Ask about this find, confirm availability, see the current listing
- “Hand-restored” only when it is documented for that individual item

### Avoid until verified

- “Delivery,” “white-glove,” “3–5 day delivery,” “free delivery,” or “curbside delivery”
- “Every piece is restored” or “fully restored” as a universal claim
- “Authentic 1970s” as a universal claim if the inventory spans eras or includes uncertain dates
- “Available” without a current inventory-status check
- Ratings, review totals, “verified buyer,” engagement counts, and customer-home claims without records
- “Shop” if the website does not provide direct ecommerce checkout; use “Explore,” “See current finds,” “Ask about this find,” or “View on Marketplace” instead

## 10. FAQ Copy

Use only answers that have been confirmed. These are V1-safe default answers, with bracketed areas requiring final policy approval.

### Where can I see the newest finds?

> The freshest active listings are usually on Facebook Marketplace, with previews and new-drop moments on Instagram. The website features a curated selection and is updated as often as possible. If you have your eye on something, send a message to confirm availability.

### How do I know whether a piece is still available?

> Because every Atomic Find is one of one, availability can change quickly. Check the active Marketplace listing or use the product inquiry button to ask Jennyfer before making plans.

### Where are you located?

> Atomic Finds ATX is based in Austin, Texas. Pickup details are shared after a purchase or pickup plan is confirmed.

### Do you offer delivery?

> Atomic Finds ATX is currently pickup by arrangement. [CONFIRM: If Jenny permits mover recommendations, add: “If you need help moving a larger piece, ask us whether we have a local recommendation.”]

### Do you offer white-glove delivery?

> No. Atomic Finds ATX does not provide white-glove delivery service.

### What styles do you specialize in?

> We are especially drawn to vintage rattan, bamboo, cane, woven textures, and character-rich furniture—often from the 1960s and 1970s—but the collection follows a good find wherever it leads.

### Are all pieces truly vintage?

> Most Atomic Finds are vintage pieces selected for their materials, shape, and personality. When an exact maker or era is known, it will be included in the listing. When it is an estimate, we will say so.

### What condition are the pieces in?

> Vintage means each piece comes with some history. We share a condition summary, measurements, and any notable wear, repairs, or preparation details with the listing. Please review the photos and notes carefully and ask questions before purchase.

### Are all pieces restored?

> Preparation varies by piece. Each listing notes relevant cleaning, conditioning, repair, or restoration work when applicable. We do not use a one-size-fits-all condition description, because every find is different.
