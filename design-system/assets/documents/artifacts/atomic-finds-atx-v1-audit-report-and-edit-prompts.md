# Atomic Finds ATX — V1 Website Audit Report & Edit Prompts

**Audited materials**

- `Atomic_Finds_ATX_Brand_Bible_V1.pdf`
- `Atomic_Finds_ATX_Website_Creative_Brief_Anthony.pdf`
- Pilot homepage source: `index.html` / `pilot.atomicfindsatx.store`

**Audit purpose**

Identify what the pilot site communicates today, distinguish confirmed brand direction from unverified operational claims, prioritize launch-blocking corrections, and provide ready-to-use prompts/instructions for edits and documentation.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Atomic Finds ATX has a strong and differentiated brand concept: curated vintage furniture with warmth, personality, story, and a recognizable “tiny time machines” world. The pilot supports that concept well through named finds, styled-room imagery, a clear color system, Nacho as a light-touch guide, and a Marketplace bridge.

The launch risk is not primarily visual. The pilot currently presents unverified or conflicting operational claims as settled facts. In particular, it repeatedly promises delivery and white-glove delivery even though the project notes state that Atomic Finds ATX does **not** offer delivery. It also publishes specific restoration, inventory, review, rating, policy, schedule, and product-provenance claims that require confirmation before launch.

**Recommended V1 launch model:**

> Atomic Finds ATX is the branded showroom, curated inventory preview, trust builder, and inquiry hub. Facebook Marketplace remains the live-listing and transaction channel during Phase 1.

The site should not represent itself as a fully live e-commerce store until product status, listing links, sales policies, and update ownership are reliable.

---

## 2. Confirmed Brand Direction

The following direction is supported by the brand Bible and creative brief:

- Curated 1960s–1970s vintage furniture with personality, especially rattan, bamboo, and character-rich pieces.
- “Tiny Time Machines for Your Home” is the central brand idea.
- The tone should be warm, curious, witty, colorful, polished, and never corporate or overly precious.
- The visual world is mid-century atomic plus 1970s boho/rattan: burnt orange, avocado green, olive/teal, warm cream, mustard yellow, and pink as an accent only.
- Furniture remains the hero. Characters, graphics, starbursts, and patterns should support rather than compete with inventory.
- Nacho is a supporting guide/mascot; the aliens are supporting “Atomic Inspection Team” details, stamps, tags, and Easter eggs.
- Each find can be named and given a concise story. The desired customer experience is discovery/adoption rather than a generic resale transaction.
- The long-term “Atomic Showroom” concept uses styled, lived-in rooms to show inventory in context.
- Instagram is intended for storytelling and community; Facebook Marketplace is intended for inventory conversion and sales.

## 3. Current Pilot Model

### What the homepage currently does

The pilot includes:

- Navigation for Shop Collection, Our Story, Showroom, Contact, and Marketplace.
- A hero with the “Tiny Time Machines” positioning and a collection CTA.
- A founder/story section focused on Jennyfer’s sourcing and restoration.
- Featured product cards for Sunny, Flora, and Agnes, including price, dimensions, condition, origin/provenance, inquiry buttons, and Marketplace buttons.
- A Nacho personality section.
- A styled-home/showroom gallery.
- Instagram-style promotional cards.
- Customer review cards and a rating summary.
- An FAQ section covering pickup, delivery, vintage condition, holds, listings, and returns.
- Footer contact information, hours, social links, a Marketplace profile link, newsletter UI, and policy links.

### What this means strategically

The pilot is already functioning as a hybrid of:

1. **Brand showroom** — introduces Atomic Finds’ perspective, personality, and visual universe.
2. **Curated inventory preview** — highlights a small set of pieces rather than a complete catalog.
3. **Trust and proof layer** — founder story, restoration language, customer reviews, and styled-room images.
4. **Marketplace bridge** — routes visitors to Jennyfer’s Facebook Marketplace profile.
5. **Inquiry layer** — routes some product CTAs to the contact page.

That is a practical V1 architecture, but only if every public claim and CTA reflects the actual operating model.

## 4. Launch-Blocking Findings

### A. Delivery and white-glove service conflict — Critical

**Current pilot claims include:**

- “Free local pickup & 3–5 day delivery in Austin, TX” in the announcement bar.
- “White-Glove Local Austin Delivery” in the marquee.
- “Free Pickup or Local White-Glove Delivery” on Sunny’s product details.
- “Free Pickup or White-Glove Local Delivery” on Flora’s product details.
- FAQ language offering local curbside drop-off for a fee.
- FAQ introduction that references Austin delivery.
- Footer wording: “Inquiries & Delivery.”

**Known project context:** Delivery and white-glove delivery are not offered.

**Required action:** Remove or replace every delivery/white-glove reference before launch.

**Safe temporary replacement:**

> Austin pickup by arrangement. Message Jennyfer to confirm availability and coordinate pickup details.

**Optional replacement if Jenny confirms mover recommendations are acceptable:**

> Atomic Finds ATX does not provide delivery or white-glove service. If you need help moving a larger piece, ask us for local mover recommendations.

Do not publish the optional statement unless Jenny confirms it.

---

### B. Restoration scope is overclaimed — High

**Current pilot language includes:**

- “Every piece is hand-picked and restored by Jennyfer.”
- “Jennyfer sources every piece herself, then restores each one by hand.”
- “Every piece is…brought back to life before it ever reaches the shop floor.”
- Individual claims such as “Hand-restored by Jennyfer” and “Cleaned, oiled, and restored by Jennyfer.”

**Risk:** These statements imply full restoration on every piece. They should not remain unless that is consistently true and Jenny is comfortable standing behind it.

**Recommended default wording until confirmed:**

> Each find is carefully selected, inspected, and prepared for its next home. Condition, vintage wear, cleaning, repairs, and restoration work are noted on each individual listing.

**Required confirmation:** Determine whether Jenny regularly cleans, oils, repairs, refinishes, paints, reupholsters, or only selectively restores items.

---

### C. Marketplace conversion path is incomplete — High

Every featured “View on Marketplace” CTA appears to route to one general Marketplace profile URL rather than the individual listing for Sunny, Flora, or Agnes.

**Risk:** Visitors have to search a personal account for the product, may see unrelated listings, and may find that the piece has already sold. This also creates a mismatch between site availability labels and Marketplace reality.

**Required action:** For each featured item, choose one:

- Add the item’s specific active Marketplace listing URL.
- Use a product-specific inquiry form/button: “Ask about Sunny.”
- Remove the item from Available Finds and move it to an Adopted/Sold archive.

**Recommended availability labels:**

- Available
- Pending
- Adopted
- Archive
- Coming Soon
- Needs Verification

Avoid “Available in Austin” unless the availability has been checked recently.

---

### D. Product claims require source verification — High

The pilot contains specific information for Sunny, Flora, Agnes, Amelia, and Ramona, including approximate years, prices, dimensions, origin/materials, condition, restoration work, source locations, and descriptions.

Examples currently shown:

- Sunny: c. 1974 coffee table, $485, 44W × 22D × 17H, Solihiya/Philippines, excellent vintage wear.
- Flora: c. 1978 bamboo dining set, $640, 42-inch diameter × 29H, four barrel armchairs, fully restored/oiled.
- Agnes: c. 1972 woven console, $385, 48W × 16D × 30H, split cane lattice, sourced from a Hyde Park estate sale.

**Required action:** Mark every existing site item internally as one of the following before it is public:

- Verified live item
- Verified sold/archive item
- Real item with incomplete data
- Visual placeholder/mock product
- Remove from website

No specific price, era, dimension, source/provenance, condition, or restoration claim should go live without a source record.

---

### E. Review and rating claims require verification — High

The homepage displays “Jennyfer Gomez · 5.0 · 81 ratings · Highly Rated” and presents named testimonials as “Verified Buyer” reviews.

**Risk:** Review aggregations, buyer verification labels, ratings, and quotes must be traceable to their source. The current pilot does not provide a source, date, link, or internal documentation record.

**Required action:** Before launch:

- Verify whether the 5.0 rating and 81 ratings are accurate and identify the source platform.
- Verify each quote against the original Marketplace feedback, message, or other source.
- Confirm that quoting the reviewer is permitted and use the minimum appropriate identifier.
- Remove “Verified Buyer” unless it reflects a verifiable source designation.
- Remove the aggregate rating until it is confirmed.

**Safe temporary alternative:**

> Kind words from Atomic Finds customers

Use only verified individual quotes.

---

### F. Policies, hours, and contact details require confirmation — High

The pilot currently states or implies:

- South Austin pickup.
- Appointment-only availability Tuesday–Saturday, 10 a.m.–6 p.m.
- Holds up to 24 hours after deposit or full payment.
- Sales generally final after pickup or delivery.
- A process for unexpected undisclosed issues.
- Public email: `atomicfindsatx@gmail.com`.
- Public phone: `865-244-8130`.

**Required action:** Confirm every statement before publishing. Do not publish pickup-area details, hours, deposit rules, phone numbers, or buyer policies just because they appear in a draft.

---

### G. Social and newsletter content may be mock content — Medium/High

The pilot displays Instagram-style cards with specific engagement counts and captions, such as 184, 219, and 305 likes. The footer also shows a newsletter subscription form with `onsubmit="return false"`, meaning it currently does not submit to a mailing platform.

**Required action:**

- Use actual embedded or manually curated posts with correct post links—or label the section as a gallery rather than a live social feed.
- Remove engagement counts unless they are authentic and intentionally maintained.
- Hide the newsletter form until it connects to a real list/automation and has a consent/privacy workflow.

---

### H. Asset and UI issues — Medium

- The source code includes a TODO noting a missing third Flora image (`product-8.png`).
- Flora’s carousel indicators show three slides even though the third visual was removed.
- Multiple CTA labels vary: “Interested?”, “Adopt me!”, and “Contact for purchase.” This can be charming, but the interaction should remain predictable.
- The pilot includes bilingual content. Spanish translation needs a separate review for fluent, consistent, accurate customer-facing copy once English content is finalized.

## 5. Recommended Sales Model

### Phase 1: Showroom + Marketplace bridge

**Purpose of the website**

Atomic Finds ATX’s website is not yet a full e-commerce store. It is the owned brand home for the story, curation, selected inventory, customer confidence, and inquiry flow that Marketplace cannot fully provide.

**Website responsibilities**

- Explain the Atomic Finds ATX point of view and inventory focus.
- Show a small, manually maintained selection of current pieces.
- Show an archive of adopted/sold pieces and styled spaces.
- Provide product context: name, photographs, category, materials, dimensions, condition, price when confirmed, and current status.
- Link visitors to a specific Marketplace listing or a monitored product-specific inquiry path.
- Explain the confirmed pickup and buying process.
- Build Instagram/newsletter audiences only when those systems are active.

**Facebook Marketplace responsibilities**

- Host real-time active listings.
- Carry the most practical listing information: title, price, dimensions, condition, pickup details, buyer messages, and sales status.
- Remain the sales/conversion channel while Jenny uses her personal Marketplace profile.

**Instagram responsibilities**

- Build awareness and repeat attention.
- Announce drops, share process, show room styling, introduce named pieces, show sold/adopted celebrations, and invite people to follow or browse current listings.

### Phase 1 customer flow

1. A buyer finds Atomic Finds via Instagram, Google, a direct link, or Marketplace.
2. The website establishes taste, credibility, and product context.
3. The buyer sees a featured item.
4. The buyer either opens the specific Marketplace listing or uses “Ask about this find.”
5. Jenny confirms availability and coordinates the confirmed purchase/pickup process.
6. Once sold, the item is marked Adopted and retained only as an archive/story piece if desired.

### Phase 2 options — decide later

- Dedicated Facebook Page and clearer business identity.
- Dedicated furniture-only Marketplace setup, if practical within Facebook’s current account rules.
- Google Business Profile.
- Direct checkout, deposits, or ecommerce inventory.
- Email/SMS launch alerts.
- Formal sourcing, styling, or restoration services.
- Automated inventory feed once the SKU/inventory system is stable.

## 6. Documentation System to Build

### Inventory record: required fields

For each item, maintain:

- SKU / internal ID
- Public item name
- Status
- Category and subcategory
- Date acquired
- Source / acquisition notes
- Approximate era and confidence level
- Maker/designer, if known
- Materials
- Dimensions
- Weight or loading notes, if appropriate
- Condition summary
- Defects/vintage wear disclosure
- Work completed: cleaned, oiled, repaired, restored, etc.
- Price
- Purchase cost and restoration cost, if used internally
- Marketplace listing URL
- Website URL/status
- Instagram post URL/status
- Photo folder/link
- Primary photo
- Date listed
- Date sold
- Buyer photo/release status
- Notes

### Review record: required fields

For each quote used publicly, maintain:

- Reviewer name or approved identifier
- Source platform
- Original review date
- Exact original quote
- URL, screenshot, or preserved source evidence
- Purchase/reference context if known
- Permission/approval status
- Where it appears: website, Instagram, email, print, etc.
- Publication date

### Content release record: required fields

- Name of owner/customer
- Contact method
- Content description
- Asset folder/location
- Permission date
- Allowed channels
- Credit/tag instructions
- Restrictions
- Revocation/contact notes

## 7. Edit Prompts

Use the following prompts with a coding assistant, designer, copywriter, or internal implementation process. Replace bracketed variables with verified information only.

### Prompt: remove delivery claims

```text
Audit the Atomic Finds ATX website for every user-facing reference to delivery, local delivery, curbside delivery, white-glove delivery, delivery timelines, or “Inquiries & Delivery.” Remove those claims from the announcement bar, marquee, product specs, product cards, FAQs, footer, metadata, and translations.

Atomic Finds ATX is pickup-only unless a future policy says otherwise. Replace the removed language with the approved copy:
“Austin pickup by arrangement. Message Jennyfer to confirm availability and coordinate pickup details.”

Change “Inquiries & Delivery” to “Inquiries & Pickup.” Do not add mover recommendations or delivery alternatives unless a separately approved policy is supplied. Keep English and Spanish content aligned.
```

### Prompt: normalize product CTA behavior

```text
Refactor Featured Collection product actions so every product card uses a consistent, status-aware purchase flow.

For status = Available:
- Primary CTA: “Ask About This Find” and prefill the contact form with the product name/SKU.
- Secondary CTA: “View on Marketplace” only when an item-specific verified Marketplace listing URL exists.

For status = Pending:
- Replace purchase CTA with “Currently Pending” and an optional “Join the Next Drop” CTA.

For status = Adopted or Archive:
- Remove price and purchase CTA.
- Display “Adopted” and use a “See Similar Finds” CTA.

Do not link every product to a general Marketplace profile. Add a content field for `marketplace_url` and hide the Marketplace CTA if it is blank. Preserve the existing visual design and bilingual attributes.
```

### Prompt: reduce unsupported restoration claims

```text
Replace global claims that every Atomic Finds ATX piece is fully restored by hand with more precise, operationally safe language.

Approved default copy:
“Each find is carefully selected, inspected, and prepared for its next home. Condition, vintage wear, cleaning, repairs, and restoration work are noted on each individual listing.”

Keep specific restoration statements only where the product record has verified work-completed details. Add product fields for `preparation_notes` and `restoration_notes` so condition and work can be described per item rather than universally.
```

### Prompt: review/rating compliance cleanup

```text
Remove the aggregate rating statement “Jennyfer Gomez · 5.0 · 81 ratings · Highly Rated” until a documented, current source is provided.

Replace the section label with “Kind Words from Atomic Finds Customers.” Keep only testimonials that have a record containing the original source, date, exact quote, and approval status. Remove “Verified Buyer” unless it is a documented designation from the source platform. Add a data structure for testimonial source and verification status.
```

### Prompt: newsletter readiness

```text
The footer newsletter form is currently non-functional. Either connect it to the approved email platform with consent language, success/error states, and a privacy-policy link, or hide the form until the integration is ready.

Do not present a Subscribe CTA that does not collect or store submissions. Preserve the visual layout if hiding the form by replacing it with “Follow new drops on Instagram and Facebook Marketplace.”
```

### Prompt: product data architecture

```text
Create a reusable product data model for Atomic Finds ATX. Required fields: sku, public_name, status, category, era, era_confidence, materials, dimensions, price, condition_summary, wear_notes, preparation_notes, restoration_notes, acquisition_notes, listing_url, marketplace_url, instagram_url, primary_image, gallery_images, date_listed, date_sold, and archive_story.

Build product cards from this model. Do not display a field if it has not been verified. Use status to control labels, purchase actions, pricing visibility, and Marketplace links.
```

### Prompt: FAQ redesign

```text
Replace the existing FAQ content with pickup-only, fact-checked answers. Keep the accordion format but reduce its visual density and ensure it is accessible with keyboard navigation and correct aria-expanded states.

Include only approved FAQs covering: where new finds are posted, how to confirm availability, pickup by arrangement, vintage condition, product details, holds/deposits if confirmed, return/final-sale policy if confirmed, and care notes. Remove delivery questions unless delivery becomes a real service.
```
