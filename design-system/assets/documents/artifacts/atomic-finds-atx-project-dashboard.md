## Photo Naming Standard

Use this format whenever possible:

`AFATX_[item-id]_[short-item-name]_[view-or-detail]_[YYYY-MM-DD].jpg`

Example:

`AFATX_024_Rattan-Peacock-Chair_Front_2026-08-30.jpg`

Suggested views:

- `Front`
- `Back`
- `Side`
- `Detail`
- `Damage`
- `Dimensions`
- `Styled`
- `Before`
- `After`

## Design Notes: Defer Until Content Is Ready

- Beige and green are currently prominent on the homepage; revisit whether they support the final brand direction.
- Replace the tropical/floral backdrop with an intentional pattern or texture system.
- The hero needs a stronger direction; consider a larger, brighter image with a clean/removed background or a simpler static treatment.
- Assign recurring patterns to specific sections rather than using motifs decoratively without a system.
- Decide how the peacock, cowboy boots, star grid, and botanical motifs should each function in the visual identity.
- Ensure the final aesthetic supports the broader inventory plan rather than limiting the brand to bamboo and rattan.
